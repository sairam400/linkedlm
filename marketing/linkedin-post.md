# LinkedIn launch post

I built something for the moment between having an idea and knowing how to say it.

Meet LinkedLM — an AI writing copilot for people who want to share useful ideas on LinkedIn without sounding like everyone else.

Start with a rough thought. Choose your voice and goal. LinkedLM turns it into a clear, thoughtful draft you can refine and make your own.

What it already does:

→ Turns a topic into a structured post
→ Adapts to your tone and content goal
→ Suggests fresh ideas when you are stuck
→ Previews and scores the draft before you share
→ Works locally, with an optional live AI mode

The goal is simple: less time staring at a blank page, more ideas worth sharing.
